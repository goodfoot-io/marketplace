# Principle 6 — Information scent at every click

Scope: why every label must predict its destination. Explanation mode.

Information scent is the set of cues that lets a reader predict what lies behind a link, heading, or hub section before committing. Weak scent causes wasted clicks, backtracking, and abandonment — even of accurate content; strong scent is what makes a correct page findable.

## In a repo

The first line of each `README` is the scope promise; directory and file names are link labels; headings are signposts; commit subjects are the scent on history. The concerns: **titles**, **link labels**, **summaries and previews**, **headings**, **disambiguation** (separating the easily-confused), and **cost of click** (shortening paths to high-value content). A reader greps names and headings before opening files, so scent must live in the names themselves.
