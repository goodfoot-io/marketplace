# Text layout / structure

- Front-load: conclusion in the first two lines; key terms at the left edge of each block. Readers scan in an F-pattern — they read the beginning and skim the rest. Structure for scanning.
- "People don't read" is false; "people won't dig for your point" is the rule. Don't cut substance — reposition it.
- Headings and link text must predict their content; readers navigate by scent.

## Landing-page anatomy

A landing page is the exception to one-message-one-stage: a sequence of one-stage blocks in funnel order, each following its stage file's rules.

- Hero = attention: task-anchored headline, informative visual (real product output), credibility signals — this is where they are spent.
- Next scroll = evaluation: delta from the reader's current workflow; scoped claims including where it doesn't fit.
- Proof = trust: real outputs, provenance, peer-specific social proof — no trust adjectives.
- CTA = trial: time-to-first-success on the reader's own material; reversibility stated.
- One page, one audience. If it must serve two (developer + buyer), split paths in the first viewport — never blend the narratives.
